\section*{Scope of Version 1.0}

This paper presents Ver. 1.0 of the Conscious Cognitive Architecture (CCA), a language-specialized formulation of the broader research framework. It focuses on how multimodal sensory input, experience- and knowledge-based memory representations, and contextual coherence support interpretation, goal-conditioned action selection, and linguistic response generation. Future versions may extend this framework toward language-independent situational awareness (Ver. 2.0) or affective and motivational dynamics (Ver. 3.0). These extensions are outside the scope of the present paper; its claims should therefore be understood as applying to the Ver. 1.0 formulation.
